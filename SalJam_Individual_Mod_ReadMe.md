## Individual Mods

### SalJam_Uranium_Collector_QOL
> Increases slots to 12 and extends the active radius from 2000 to 17500 units, so you can clean up the bodies.

### SalJam_SuperTeslaCoil
> This adds a new Super Tesla Coil. Its bigger and more powerful, dealing 500 electric damage instead of 100. It has higher power requirements though.

### SalJam_Repair_Bench
> Adds a repair bench that only uses electricity for repairs, but repairing will take time. Includes an alteration bench for removing attachments in 1 second, as these cant be repaired.
> Fixed issue caused by meshable table being changed by Devs.
> Took the opportunity to remove Workshop items, as I found once they had been repaired, you couldnt take them back to the space station.

### SalJam_Roaming_NPCs
> Added random spawns of TPS soldiers over Elysiums map. Some will drop higher end loot.
